<section class="home-section research-story">
  <h2>Research</h2>

  <div class="research-story__body">
    <p>I work with Prof. Dinesh Manocha in the <a href="https://gamma.umd.edu/">GAMMA Lab</a> on learning-based human-aware autonomy. I am interested in how robots can anticipate human intent, reason about how their own actions will affect people, and proactively adapt their behavior during interaction. My research interests span robot learning, embodied AI, social navigation, and human-robot interaction.</p>

    <p>I am interested in combining multimodal perception with learned models, including vision-language and vision-language-action models, to support decisions during human-robot interaction. A central question for me is how robots can learn efficiently from human-human interactions and limited human-robot interaction data. I hope to apply these methods in shared environments, including construction, search-and-rescue, healthcare, and assistive robotics.</p>

    <p>Previously, at Swarthmore, I worked with <a href="https://www.sukritv.com/">Prof. Sukrit Venkatagiri</a> on human-centered AI safety. I developed <a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3772318.3791313">ScamPilot</a> and contributed to Privacy Navigator, studying how AI systems can help people recognize and respond to online harms. That work shaped my interest in evaluating safety around real human behavior. I now bring that perspective to robots operating around people.</p>
  </div>
